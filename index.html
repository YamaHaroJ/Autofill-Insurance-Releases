FAQ

Good to know.

Does Autofill make decisions or submit applications for me?

Autofill assists with supported workflows. Agents remain responsible for reviewing customer information, verifying entries, and deciding what to submit.

How does cloud transcription work?

Available cloud transcription features process audio through Autofill's cloud service. Cloud processing may use wallet credits; check the in-product cost information before starting.

Will it work with every insurance carrier or website?

No universal compatibility is promised. Autofill features depend on the forms and workflows currently supported by the extension.

Where can I get help?

Autofill is a productivity tool and does not provide insurance, legal, tax, or financial advice. AI-generated transcripts and extracted fields may be inaccurate and must be reviewed. Do not rely on automated output without verifying it against the source information and applicable requirements.
